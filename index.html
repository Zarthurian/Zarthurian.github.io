Who Are Zarthurian productions? What's a "Zarthurian"? And why does this website remind me of Ceefax?

Zathurian Productions is an independent developer (that means that it's a one-man operation) and created some shows, short films and even some games you may recognise like "Induction Day".

Zarthurian (the name) was originally created in 2019 when I first joined Roblox. It was a combination of three things: my first name, Zachary; The kid from the Aquaman movie, Arthur; and a mispelling of the word "Atlantean", Atlantian. So that makes it Zachary Arthur Atlantian. That name would be contrated and has been used for all of my other online names.

though to answer your question about the website's theme, this design came to me in a dream.

The future:

New game being announced based on a 1990s novella

There is going to be a point and click adventure game based on the book "The thief of always" by Clive Barker, the difference being that instead of time being faster on the outside, time is faster on the inside.

The re-used characters are Carl (charlie) Falazcat, Harriet Pretorius, Jaques (Finley) Falazcat. These characters were re-ued from Stephen's Holiday Camp and Induction Day. Philippe the flamingo will return from being scrapped from one of the beta versions of Stephen's Holiday camp. The original character is Margotte Peony, the recycled character is Philippe Flamingo and the borrowed character is Old Man Jim.
